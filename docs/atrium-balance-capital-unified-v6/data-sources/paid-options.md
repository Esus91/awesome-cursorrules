# Best Paid Data Options

## Top-tier institutional options
1. **Bloomberg (Terminal + Enterprise Data License)**
   - Broadest cross-asset + news + reference + analytics.
   - Best for institutional-grade coverage and compliance-heavy workflows.
2. **Refinitiv / LSEG Data & Analytics**
   - Strong fundamentals, estimates, ownership, and global coverage.
3. **S&P Global Market Intelligence / Capital IQ**
   - Excellent company fundamentals, comps, private/public datasets.
4. **FactSet**
   - Strong fundamentals + portfolio analytics + workstation/API ecosystem.
5. **Morningstar Direct / Office / Data Feeds**
   - Strong fund, ETF, and portfolio analytics content.

## Developer-first paid APIs (lower cost than terminals)
1. **Polygon.io paid plans** — high-frequency market data and broad APIs.
2. **Intrinio** — modular fundamentals, options, real-time packages.
3. **Tiingo paid** — affordable EOD + IEX-derived feeds.
4. **Quodd / dxFeed / Barchart** — real-time feeds and derivatives coverage.

## Selection criteria
- Asset classes needed (equities only vs multi-asset).
- Required latency (EOD vs intraday vs tick).
- Redistribution rights and legal terms.
- Historical depth and survivorship-bias handling.
- SLA, support, and uptime commitments.

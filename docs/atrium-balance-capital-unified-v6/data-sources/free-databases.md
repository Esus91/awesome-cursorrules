# Free Databases / APIs for Investment & Company Data

## Best free options
1. **SEC EDGAR** (official filings, company facts, submissions) — best for U.S. regulatory fundamentals.
2. **FRED (St. Louis Fed)** — macroeconomic time series (rates, inflation, unemployment).
3. **NASDAQ Data Link free datasets** — selective free market and alternative datasets.
4. **IEX Cloud free-tier/community endpoints (if currently available)** — selected U.S. quote/reference data.
5. **Alpha Vantage free tier** — stock/FX/crypto time series with call limits.
6. **Financial Modeling Prep free tier** — limited fundamental/market endpoints.
7. **Polygon free tier (if active)** — limited delayed/historical access.
8. **Tiingo free tier** — limited daily EOD access.
9. **World Bank Open Data** — macro and country indicators.
10. **OECD Data API** — economic indicators and forecasts.

## Practical usage by layer
- **Regulatory truth**: SEC EDGAR.
- **Macro context**: FRED + World Bank + OECD.
- **Market prices**: One of Alpha Vantage / FMP / Tiingo / Polygon based on limits.
